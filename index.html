Some cats may be safer for people with cat allergies than others. But no cat is 100% hypoallergenic, because all cats produce a protein called Fel d 1 in their saliva, skin glands, and urine. Different breeds have different amounts of this protein.
  • When cats groom themselves, their saliva containing Fel d 1 dries on their fur and skin
  • This dried residue turns into lightweight dust called dander that floats in the air.
  • The allergy symptoms come from this dander, not the fur itself.
